Stops sit at their real locations, and each of the 18 links follows real streets with its driving length (routing by OSRM, OpenStreetMap data). A* searches over these links. With several drop-offs, every visiting order is costed with A* and the cheapest is used; Greedy and BFS follow the same order. The chosen traffic level shifts every link up or down a level, and a higher delay risk makes congested links cost more. The network is separate from the city type above.
